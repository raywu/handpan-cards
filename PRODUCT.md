## Register

product

## Users

Handpan players learning chords and progressions for their own instrument and scale, mostly on a phone while playing, also on tablet, desktop and printed cards. Readers of the learning pages (scale hub, 16 scale pages, Handpan 101, About, 404) arrive from search.

## Product purpose

Handpan Theory is a free chord-card system for handpans: a flashcard and progression app plus printable poker-size cards, built from one verified data source. It teaches which chords a given scale offers and how to play them.

## Brand

Name: Handpan Theory, full name, untranslated, never abbreviated. Identity: the Two contours mark, one unchanged master (ivory on warm black, ink on light surfaces) with the full-name wordmark. Violet belongs to actions, links and states, never the logo or protected instrument colors.

Roles: canvas #0F0E0B, paper #F6F4ED, field #FFFFFF, ink #242424, muted #5E5850, border #8A847B, action #6756B5. Nunito Sans for UI and reading; protected Marcellus (chord and progression names) and Bitter (note lines) stay. Base spacing 4/8/14/22; compact root header 64px.

## Surfaces and modes

- Operate: practice (warm dark, card dominant), the mode menu, Add/Edit Scale and Layout forms.
- Read: scale hub, scale pages, Handpan 101, About overlay, 404 (light ground, 16px/1.5 body proposed).

Briefs: `docs/plans/assets/2026-11-02-field-guide/surface-briefs.md`.

## Anti-references

Contour decorations around controls; tinted diagrams or replaced card colors; the logo as a watermark; a second simplified favicon symbol; a dark header band; decorative violet fills; new navigation from the reference image.

## Design principles

1. Keep the card dominant; never shrink a card to make room for branding.
2. Practice stays warm dark; menus, forms and reading use light ground.
3. Inactive controls stay neutral; focus is visibly distinct from selection.
4. Preserve every protected renderer, palette, font and geometry.
5. One master across logo and favicon.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Existing localization (seven tables, CJK fallbacks), keyboard and focus behaviour, 44px touch targets and the language selector stay functional. Token pairs in `token-check.json` meet 4.5:1 text and 3:1 non-text.
