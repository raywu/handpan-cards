D Amara 9 handpan chords

Amara has a minor feel and no half step above the fifth. Start with the first three cards: play each card's lit notes, then move to the next. The app has 27 chord cards for this scale. New to handpan? Read Handpan 101: how to play a handpan first.

Also called Celtic, or Celtic Minor.

A little history

D Amara is built from D natural minor with the sixth note left out: D A C D E F G A C. Isthmus Instruments says the names Celtic and Amara are mostly used interchangeably, and that almost every maker has a version of it. It calls the scale intuitive for beginner players. MAG Instruments ties both names to D natural minor and lists them among the most popular handpan scales. Some makers sell it as D Celtic Minor.

How to read a chord card

  1. The darker colour is the root; the lighter colour, the other chord tones.
  2. The line under the pan names the notes, root first, with their field numbers.
