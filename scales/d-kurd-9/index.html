Kurd is natural minor over the ding. Start with the i, iv and v: play each card's lit notes, then move to the next. The app has 49 chord cards for this scale. New to handpan? Read Handpan 101: how to play a handpan first.

Also called Kurdish minor.

A little history

D Kurd 9 is the plain D minor Kurd layout: a D ding with eight notes around it, A Bb C D E F G A. Saraz Handpans lists this exact set for D minor and says the name comes from Arabic music, short for Kurd Maqam. It also says Victor Levinson, who builds the SPB, first popularised the scale on handpans, and that viral videos spread D minor pans further.

Popularized by
Victor Levinson, through his SPB pans, and viral videos.

How to read a chord card

  1. The darker colour is the root; the lighter colour, the other chord tones.
  2. The line under the pan names the notes, root first, with their field numbers.
