D Aegean 18 handpan chords

Aegean is a bright, open major sound with a raised fourth. Start on the ding, D, and use the I chord first: play each card's lit notes, then move to the next. The app has 62 chord cards for this scale. New to handpan? Read Handpan 101: how to play a handpan first.

Also called the Aegean Lydian scale.

A little history

D Aegean is built on the Lydian mode. MAG Instruments explains that the Lydian sound comes from raising the 4th note by a half step. On a D pan that gives D E F# G# A B C#. The same page says the Aegean is a major scale whose sound falls between major and minor, and that it is named for the Aegean Sea.

How to read a chord card

  1. The darker colour is the root; the lighter colour, the other chord tones.
  2. The line under the pan names the notes, root first, with their field numbers.
  3. Orange U numbers and dashed circles are bottom-shell notes, played from inside the pan.
