F# Serenity handpan scale (Neotone Mutant)

F# Serenity is a scale by Reo Matsumoto for the Neotone Mutant: ding F#3, then nine top-shell notes (A3 B3 C#4 E4 F#4 A4 B4 C#5 E5) and nine mutant notes, laid out by field below.

About the scale

F# Serenity is a scale by Reo Matsumoto, shown in his video The Serenity Scale.

Reo Matsumoto is a handpan musician and beatboxer from Japan who teaches handpan workshops (Master the Handpan).

In his own profile, as we read it, he says he believes everything has spirit and plays with love and spirit (Master the Handpan). Instruments du Monde describes his music as music without borders that mixes genres. That is his general approach, not a statement about this scale.

Questions

What notes does the F# Serenity handpan scale use?

F# Serenity on a Neotone Mutant uses ding F#3; top shell fields 1 to 9: A3 B3 C#4 E4 F#4 A4 B4 C#5 E5; mutant notes: D3 G#3 E3 B2 A5 F#5 C#6 B5 E6.

Who created F# Serenity?

F# Serenity is a scale by Reo Matsumoto, created by him and shared in his video The Serenity Scale.

Which handpan is the F# Serenity layout for?

It is laid out for a Neotone Mutant: a ding, nine top-shell fields and nine mutant notes.
