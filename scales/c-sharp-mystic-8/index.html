This pan has no iv or v chord card, so start with the i chords: play each card's lit notes, then move to the next. The app has 19 chord cards for this scale. New to handpan? Read Handpan 101: how to play a handpan first.

A little history

C# Mystic is a six-note scale: C# D# E G# A B. That is C# natural minor with the F# left out. MAG Instruments lists it as C# G# A C# D# E G# B and calls it a hexatonic minor scale, close to their Integral but with a different last note. The same page says the very low start and very high end give it a mystical, dark mood.

How to read a chord card

  1. The darker colour is the root; the lighter colour, the other chord tones.
  2. The line under the pan names the notes, root first, with their field numbers.
