About this Neotone scale

F2 Astronaut 16 sits on the DOME+9 hotkey. It has nine main tonefields around an F2 ding and six mutant notes, each inside the tonefield it pairs with. The other three tonefields hold no mutant note.

On a chord card the main tonefields are the large circles and each mutant tonefield is the small lens inside its circle. The A#m card strikes the mutant A#2 together with the chord.

How to read a Neotone chord card

  1. The darker colour is the root; the lighter colour, the other chord tones.
  2. The line under the pan names the notes, root first, with their field numbers.
  3. Orange M numbers are mutant notes. A lit lens inside a circle is a mutant note to strike together with the chord.
