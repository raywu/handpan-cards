Also called Kurdish minor.

A little history

D Kurd is the natural minor scale (the Aeolian mode) on D. Makers say the name comes from the Arabic and Turkish maqam Kurd. That maqam starts with a half step, which makes it closer to Phrygian than to plain minor, so the name is a loose borrowing. You can hear the link from the first rim note: A Bb C D E F G is A Phrygian. Saraz Handpans credits Victor Levinson, builder of the SPB handpan, with popularising the scale and first calling his D minor pans "Kurd". Early viral videos spread it further.

Popularized by
Victor Levinson, through his SPB pans, and early viral videos.

How to read a chord card

  1. The darker colour is the root; the lighter colour, the other chord tones.
  2. The line under the pan names the notes, root first, with their field numbers.
