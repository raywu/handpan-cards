Low Pygmy is a minor sound with a deep ding. Every card here is built on the i chord, F minor: play each card's lit notes, then move to the next. The app has 11 chord cards for this scale. New to handpan? Read Handpan 101: how to play a handpan first.

Also sold as F minor Low Pygmy.

A little history

F Low Pygmy 9 puts a ding on F2, an octave below the usual F3. Eight notes sit around it on the top shell: F G Ab C Eb F G Ab. That is the five notes of F minor pentatonic. One maker lists it as an F2 Low Pygmy in F minor, with the notes F F G G# C D# F G G#. Makers differ on the exact notes and octaves, so check your own pan.

How to read a chord card

  1. The darker colour is the root; the lighter colour, the other chord tones.
  2. The line under the pan names the notes, root first, with their field numbers.
