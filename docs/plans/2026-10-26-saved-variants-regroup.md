## Learnings converted to constraints

1. Separate feature correctness, browser infrastructure and clock eligibility.
   A helper repair cannot close a product failure without matching evidence.
2. Freeze the complete candidate before costly checks. Integration after proofs
   invalidates affected proofs; integrate approved Helpers first, palettes/swipe later.
3. Freeze tests before source. Preserve actual pending browser hash; never heal it
   by restoring expected input before dispatch. Assert late cases actually ran.
4. Keep implementation equal to the reviewed truth table; require explicit review
   of every deviation (the NULL exemption demonstrated why).
5. Use a small hypothesis ledger: observation, competing explanation, discriminating
   check, exact source, expected outcomes and stop. No instrumentation fishing.
6. Retain failed data and reject unavailable evidence. Historical passes, model
   passes and owner Helpers QA do not substitute for candidate native/performance.
7. Avoid repeated broad validation during local editing; run focused relevant checks
   then one exact-head full CI. New code/failure justifies further checks, not habit.

a failed independent review.

new subplan opens a new explicitly linked cycle; do not rename work to reset counts.
