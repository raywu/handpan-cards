## Demand and differentiated content

Research query clusters by intent, not combinations of every key/maker/count:
identify exact inventory; understand available chords; learn a progression; access
bottom-note transitions; compare variants. Keep an intent registry mapping synonyms
and aliases to one primary page. “Amara”, “Celtic minor” and E minor terminology
must be explained against actual notes, not silently treated as identical products.

Candidate family guide `/learn/e-amara-chords/` compares exact inventories, explains
what missing/present C notes permit and routes readers to an appropriate lesson.
Candidate20-note lesson `/learn/e-amara-20-chord-progressions/` needs an independently
verified13+7 inventory and matching CTA; do not substitute the owner's13+5 link.
A separate18-note lesson is conditional on useful distinct exercises, otherwise
it becomes a variant section. Slugs are proposals pending hosting approval.

[Yatao's E Amara20](https://yataoshop.com/collections/handpans/products/handpan-yataopan-e-amara-20)
provides one current13+7 inventory. [Existing E Amara teaching](https://www.maltemartenmethod.com/e-amara-scale-course)
shows specific competition. These sources establish products/content, not demand,
efficacy or reuse rights. Do not copy teaching prose, examples, photos or audio.

Each eligible lesson needs accurate inventory/zone diagram plus text alternative,
at least two reviewed exercises, concrete chord tones/voicings, an explained
transition, practice instructions and one honest limitation/adaptation. The lesson
must help without opening the app. If differences reduce to names/transposition,
consolidate instead of creating thin pages. No artificial word-count target,
keyword stuffing, fake freshness, invented firsthand playing or endorsements.

[Google people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)
and [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
support usefulness/originality and identify scaled low-value/doorway risks.
[AI-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)
does not ban reviewed AI assistance or guarantee ranking. These are current
primary guidance researched2026-10-25, not approval of our specific strategy.

Alternatives: consolidated guide only (default when variants lack unique lessons),
small layout lessons (when justified), app-only (if reviewer capacity absent), or
interactive reference (new product scope, defer). Reject mass keyword/maker pages,
auto-publication and special AI-search files as substitutes for useful content.

### Match-first reader journey and usefulness gate

Concrete article skeleton: audience and prerequisites → exact octave-and-zone
inventory with accessible diagram → “does your instrument match?” check → reviewed
exercises and transitions → optional app CTA → variant/help/return links. Name
required bottom pitches and the assumed access/playing technique before exercises.
A matching family name or note count alone is insufficient: display the inventory
needed for each exercise and clearly distinguish partial compatibility.

If notes/zones differ, route to the family guide/variant comparison and explain how
to edit the instrument in the existing app; do not imply the published voicing
still applies. The app customization route is optional and requires actual-UI
copy validation; it does not silently repair an incompatible lesson. Keep a stable
article URL/bookmark and normal browser Back/open-in-new-tab choices so readers
can return to the exercise; do not overwrite the article route with the app hash.
No forced new tab or focus trap. Text note/field tables and practice steps remain
readable without diagrams, JavaScript, audio or an app click.

Before publication, a representative novice and an intermediate learner (or clearly
qualified proxy pending real readers) separately walk through the intended lesson:
identify their matching inventory; locate the exercise notes; explain bottom-note
requirements; follow or verbally demonstrate the practice sequence; explain that
the CTA loads the instrument, not that exact progression; and return to the lesson.
Actual playing claims still require a player with the relevant instrument.
Record task completion, critical confusion, device and fixes, with consent and no
published personal details. A critical task failure blocks publication/expansion
until revision/recheck. Proxy-only coverage is a limitation requiring explicit
owner acceptance, never a claim of completed real-learner validation. Search
traffic, keyboard checks and author approval cannot substitute for this gate.

## Technical architecture and measurement

Recommended option to validate: minimal static sibling HTML under `/learn/`, served
by existing hosting, no framework/runtime content dependency. Each article has a
clean indexable path, useful initial HTML, unique title/description/H1, self-canonical,
real a[href] links, accessible diagrams and page navigation back to the app.
App remains independently functional single-file/no-build. This is an **architecture
proposal**, not already permitted by current root-only sitemap tests.

Compare against an external blog/existing site before owner decision. External
publishing reduces repo coupling but adds ownership, cross-domain measurement,
link maintenance and privacy checks. A separate content repo/site is unnecessary
until actual editorial ownership/build needs justify overhead. Do not fork engine
logic or create a second parser. Static output, if generated later, must be committed
and reproducible; any new build pipeline needs explicit approval.

[Google URL guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/url-structure)
distinguishes paths from fragments: custom app hashes are reader destinations,
not separate indexable article URLs. Canonical hints do not fix thin duplicate
pages. Deliberately extend sitemap acceptance from exact-root to the approved
public article allowlist; preserve no-hash/no-query/clean-URL checks, current
no-lastmod policy unless separately changed, robots behavior and DEV noindex.
Never delete/weaken failing pins just to accommodate content. Structured data
must truthfully describe visible content; no invented ratings/FAQ/rich-result claims.

Current app analytics config strips **query and hash**, using origin+pathname.
Nine current events lack article CTA/complete arrival attribution. Same-domain
article→app clicks are not automatic outbound events; share_open suppresses repeat
instrument arrivals. Query-before-hash preserves link syntax but does not establish
measurement. On an external blog, enhanced outbound measurement could send the
full custom link: audit/disable/redact before enabling it. Do not transmit scale strings/names/hash payloads to analytics or publish private
custom-scale data, visitor identifiers or sensitive exports. Deliberately approved
public instrument inventories and their CTA links remain visible lesson content.

Separate proposed metrics: indexed page impressions/clicks; reader utility; CTA
click; instrument arrival; subsequent practice. Do not call card_flip or a counted
share_open proof of a delivered named progression. Proposed future instrumentation
uses only reviewed allowlisted article IDs/entry context, never arbitrary query
values. New analytics/app edits require separate approval and privacy/negative
tests. If missing, use aggregate GSC plus direct pilot user feedback and clearly
state that full funnel conversion is unmeasured. No raw credentials requested.
