## #174 swipe fix: auto-decisions and owner trade-off

- **Swipe-only card.** Chosen from the owner's iPhone Chrome log: iOS WebKit claimed
  the gesture under `pan-y` at ~10px of vertical drift and fired `pointercancel`.
  `main`, `.scene` and `#card` now use `touch-action:none`.
- **Thresholds unchanged** from #172 (commit 18px, fling 0.15px/ms): the lab showed the
  misses were cancels, not short swipes.
- **Axis guard added.** `swipeDecision` returns 0 when |dy| > |dx|, so a vertical drag
  no longer steps the deck.
- **eatClick.** The `setTimeout(0)` decay is kept, and it is not armed on a cancelled
  release.
- **Owner trade-off, accepted 2026-09-30.** Touches on the card and `main` cannot
  scroll the page vertically or pinch-zoom, including over `main`'s margins. The owner
  said: "The tradeoff of vertical scroll on the card could be an issue in the future but
  let's stick with this for now". Revisit if a card ever outgrows the viewport.
