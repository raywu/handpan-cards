## Recommendation and confidence

**Conditional proceed to a bounded lesson pilot design, not automatic publication.**
The opportunity is a useful, inspectable lesson for a precisely identified note
inventory, with playable chord diagrams and an optional instrument-loading link.
Its value must remain when the reader never opens the app. Do not create one page
per keyword spelling, transposition, maker or hash. Initially test one substantial
family guide and at most two distinct layout lessons, subject to source, musical
review and owner capacity gates. The three-page cap is an operating proposal,
not an SEO optimum or an authorization to publish.

These are candidate slugs, not final publishing architecture. The engine lane must
validate the supplied link, chord eligibility and article examples. Do not assume
two extra notes create enough instructional difference to merit another page.
Use one editorial intent registry mapping synonyms to the canonical lesson;
capitalize note symbols consistently with English scale-input conventions. Do not
manufacture locale pages because the app UI has translations. Language expansion
requires demand and native editorial review, with natural local naming/tone.

An eligible lesson must contain an accurate note/zone inventory, an accessible
diagram/text equivalent, at least two independently checked practice examples,
the notes each chord uses, one explained transition and one real limitation or
adaptation. The numbers are a proposed editorial minimum, not a word-count/ranking
rule. A lesson failing to add useful analysis joins the family guide or is rejected.
Engine tier labels alone do not substantiate beginner/intermediate playability.

## Alternatives and tactical operations

- **Consolidated guide first:** lowest maintenance and overlap risk, useful when
  demand is sparse or most examples transfer unchanged. Recommended default when
  evidence cannot justify a distinct scale page.
- **Small individual lessons:** better exact-instrument utility if inventories and
  exercises genuinely differ; costs musical review and link/version maintenance.
- **Interactive scale reference:** potentially useful, but new product/router scope
  and broader testing; defer until user evidence warrants it.
- **App-only:** zero content upkeep but cannot answer explanatory learning intents
  as directly. Keep this option if review capacity is absent.
- **Bulk permutations/auto-publishing:** reject for the pilot. Technical generation
  throughput does not establish teaching value or search demand.

Before approving any article, assign a named editor, a musically qualified reviewer
and a technical verifier. Root/AI cannot substitute for physical playing expertise.
Record source revision/date, inventory fingerprint, query cluster, original value,
rights status, engine/RNG provenance, resolved musical outputs, review receipts,
canonical URL, exact CTA and next review date. Use small structured facts records
and deterministic verified diagrams; keep explanatory prose reviewed separately.
No scraped maker images/audio, copied lessons, synthetic testimonials or invented
play testing. Cite inventory provenance without suggesting affiliation.

Proposed capacity exercise: time the first reviewed draft end-to-end and record
human minutes separately from agent time. Owner selects a monthly hour/cost cap
before expanding. No credible cost estimate exists yet. Recheck content when the
engine's musical output, parser/share contract, linked instrument or source claim
changes; changes invalidate the affected review, not every unrelated article.
