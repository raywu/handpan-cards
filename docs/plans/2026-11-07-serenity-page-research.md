  Another listing says "Tokyo-born" (search summary of entershibuya.com / stereofox listings) -> birthplace CONFLICT. [L]
- Began beatboxing 2005 (instruments-du-monde page above; https://ja.ra.co/dj/reomatsumoto/biography per search). [H]
- Japanese beatbox championship: 2010 (instruments-du-monde) vs 2011 (other listings in search). CONFLICT, do not state a year. [L]

  (instruments-du-monde). Festivals incl. OZORA, Fusion, Glastonbury (search summary of label.stereofox.com). [M]
- Organised Tokyo HandPan Festival, directs Tokyo HandPan Lab (instruments-du-monde). [M]

  https://hipsy.eu/event/211199-explore-the-handpan-for-beginner-by-reo-matsumoto); Dutch Handpan Festival lineup

  Yishama Pantam, Elaia Handpans, MAG Instruments (same page). [M]
