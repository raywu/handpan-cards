# Prompt: plan a redraw of the Handpan Theory mark that matches the mock

Paste everything below the line into a fresh Claude Code session on
`raywu/handpan-cards`. It asks for a PLAN backed by cheap measured spikes, not
a finished logo.

---

The Handpan Theory logo in `docs/brand/logo/` (generated by
`docs/brand/make_logo.py`) does not match the owner's brand board. Your job is
to find the fastest reliable way to redraw the mark so it matches, prove it
with a few cheap spikes, and write a plan the owner can approve. Do not
replace the shipped SVGs in this session.

## Inputs (all in the repo)

- `docs/brand/reference/brand-board.webp`: the source of truth. The

  horizontal lockup and three app icons repeat it at smaller sizes. Use them

- `docs/brand/reference/mock-mark-zoom-3x.png`: the primary mark cropped from

- `docs/brand/make_logo.py`: one parametric geometry; variants (full, single,

  is outlined from `tools/fonts`. Keep that structure (one geometry in,
  every variant out) unless you show a better one.
- Design system: https://claude.ai/artifact/93knqkDWTEUZe8iGLktZYB (tokens:
  `accent-teal #44E0D8`, `accent-gold #F5C78E`, `warm-white #F8EDD9`,
  `background #0F0E0B`).

## What is wrong (owner, 2026-10-31)

Three things, all to be fixed. Re-check each against the board yourself;
these readings were made by eye from the 3x zoom.

1. **The bottom shell is missing.** The mock's pan has three parts: a dome
   (with a raised ding ellipse on top and five tone fields in a curved row), a
   thin rim band whose two edges meet in points at the left and right ends
   (a lens, sticking out slightly past the dome), and under it a shallow
   bowl, the bottom shell, whose lowest point is roughly 40% of the dome's
   height below the rim. v1 draws the rim as a single ellipse with a thin
   sliver under it, so the pan reads as a flat disc.
2. **The ring circles do not match.** In the mock they are concentric
   circles behind the pan (about three, plus fainter partial ones), and
   their brightness changes around each circle: bright teal on the upper
   left, warm gold on the lower right, fading to almost nothing at the upper
   right and lower left, with a soft bloom where they are brightest. The
   inner ring is the brightest. The pan hides them where it overlaps, and
   they show again below the bottom shell. v1 draws evenly tinted circles
   centred on the pan with a straight gradient, which reads flat.
3. **The slanted orbit is wrong.** In the mock the orbit is one closed
   ellipse, tilted about 24 degrees (rising to the right), with the pan
   inside it. The teal dot sits at its lower-left tip and the gold dot near
   its upper-right tip. The front half runs from the teal dot under the
   bottom shell (crossing in front of it) and rises to the gold dot. The
   back half goes from the gold dot left until the dome hides it, and from
   the teal dot up to the left end of the rim, where the pan hides it. The
   minor axis is narrow. v1 tilts the orbit less, starts the line at the teal
   dot (no back arc on the left), and lets the front arc cut through the rim
   instead of passing under the bottom shell.

Approximate landmarks in board pixels (re-measure them; do not trust
them): teal dot centre (559,302); gold dot centre (783,202); ding centre
(667,218); dome top y 210; rim ends (563,258) and (773,258); rim band
lower edge at the centre y 273; bottom-shell low point (670,287); tone
fields between y 245 and 255; inner ring top (677,173); outer ring top
(677,137).

## How to work (optimise for iteration speed)

1. **Measure before you draw.** Build a landmark table from the primary
   mark: every point above, plus ring radii and centre, orbit centre, axes
   and tilt, stroke widths, dot radii, and the relative sizes of the tone
   fields. Normalise to the pan's rim width so the numbers carry to any
   output size. Cross-check against the stacked lockup and the full-colour
   app icon; where they disagree, say which you followed and why.
2. **Build a comparison harness first** (minutes, not hours). One script
   that renders a candidate SVG at the mock's scale and produces: a
   side-by-side, a 50% onion-skin overlay on the raster, and a difference
   image, plus a printed table of landmark errors (candidate vs measured,
   as % of rim width). Use the preinstalled Playwright for rendering and
   Pillow for the images. Every later step reports through this harness, so
   "matches" means numbers and an overlay, not an impression.
3. **Spike 2 to 3 approaches in parallel**, timeboxed, each judged by the
   harness. For example:
   - **A. Retune the parametric model:** add the bottom shell and lens rim,
     a closed tilted orbit with correct front/back occlusion, and rings with
     an angular brightness falloff (conic-style: per-arc segments, or
     masks), fitted to the landmark table.
   - **B. Fit curves to the raster:** edge-detect the mock, fit ellipses and
     cubic Béziers to each stroke by least squares, then rebuild them as
     clean parametric shapes. Gives measured geometry with no guessing.
   - **C. Trace and clean:** auto-trace (potrace or similar on a
     thresholded crop) as a reference skeleton only, then redraw clean
     strokes over it. Useful if A and B disagree.
   Invent a better approach if you see one. Keep each spike in a scratch
   folder; do not touch `docs/brand/logo/`.
4. **Stop the spikes as soon as one is clearly ahead**, then compare in one
   table: approach, worst landmark error, overlay verdict per problem (1, 2,
   3), how easy later tweaks are, file size, and whether it keeps "one
   geometry in, every variant out".

## Constraints

- Clean vector output: strokes and simple shapes, no embedded raster, no
  traced blobs in the shipped files. Small sizes (`mark-small`, 16-48px)
  stay legible: say what the small mark drops (rings, glow, fields) and
  keep the bottom shell and orbit readable.
- Glow and ring falloff must survive the single-colour and monochrome
  variants: those drop the gradient and glow but keep the same geometry.
- Colours come from the tokens above; no new hues.
- Changes go into the generator; never hand-edit a generated SVG.
- Nothing in `index.html`, the favicon data URI, `tools/make_icons.py`,
  `apple-touch-icon.png` or `og.png` changes in this work; adopting the new
  mark in the app is the rebrand plan's job
  (`docs/prompts/2026-10-31-handpan-theory-rebrand-plan.md`).

## The plan document
