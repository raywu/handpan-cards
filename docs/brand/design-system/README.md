Handpan Theory is the chord and chord-progression practice app for handpan players, moving from handpan.raywu.org to handpantheory.com (apex canonical; www redirects to it; Cloudflare runs the domain). The brand is a quiet, dark, elegant frame; the colour belongs to the music. Keep the chrome neutral (`background`, `surface`, `text-primary`) so the scale palettes on the chord cards stay the loudest thing on screen.

The logo set is in **Logos** (SVG, redrawn from measurements of the owner's brand board and regenerated by `docs/brand/make_logo.py` in the repo; `docs/brand/redraw/check_mark.py` checks it against the board). The board and phone mock are in **Reference**.

## Content fundamentals

- Name: **Handpan Theory** (title case, two words).
- Subtitle: the app at the site root is for chords AND chord progressions, so the lockup tagline reads **CHORDS & PROGRESSIONS**, uppercase and tracked to the wordmark's width. In running text say "chords and chord progressions" in full (page title, description, the line under the header). The board's "CHORD PROGRESSIONS" is superseded.
- Visible text says "scale", never "deck" (`D KURD 10`, not "Kurd deck").
- Calm, instructional voice; second person when guiding ("Right-left-right-right, left-right-left-left, moving the strokes across the chord's notes"). No exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Scale chips are uppercase: `D AMARA 10`, `D KURD 10`. Progressions use arrows: `Dm → F → Am → C`.
- Card copy is English-only; interface copy is translated (eight tables).

## Visual foundations

**Colour.** Set the page on `background`; header bar, buttons and chips on `surface` with a 1px `hairline` border. Text is `text-primary`; tagline and captions `text-secondary`. `accent-teal` and `accent-gold` appear only in the mark and as small signals (focus ring, the underline under the current chord in the progression). Never colour a whole control with an accent. The dark theme is primary; the light theme exists for the monochrome dark-on-light lockup and print-adjacent pages.

**Scale palettes are locked.** `scale-*-root`, `scale-*-tone` and `bottom-orange` are product data (CLAUDE.md "Design system"). The rebrand never changes them, the card anatomy, or the diagram label rule. The chord card stays `card-paper` with a root-coloured four-sided frame.

**Type.** The wordmark is set in the display family (`wordmark-header` in the app bar, `wordmark-lg` on landing and OG images). The board asks for "Playfair Display / Cormorant or similar"; Marcellus is already loaded by the app and reads close to the mock, so it is the default until an exploration proves another face. The tagline is `tagline` in the UI family. The progression line under the card is `progression` (display family). Note lines on cards stay Bitter (`note-line`).

**Spacing.** Use the app's four-step ramp: `sp-1` within a group, `sp-2` between peers, `sp-3` between groups, `sp-4` around surfaces and as the minimum clear space around a lockup.

**Shape.** Header buttons and arrow buttons are `radius-md` squares with a `hairline` border; scale chips are `radius-pill`; the active chip takes a 1.5px border in its scale's root colour and a coloured dot.

**Depth.** No drop shadows on chrome. The only glow is the mark's own teal-to-gold orbit, and it stays soft (the board's Don't panels: no saturated neon, no rainbow).

## Logo

- Files: `mark-full` (teal-to-gold orbit with soft glow, dark grounds), `mark-single` (`mark-single` ink, dark grounds), `mark-mono-dark` (light ink for dark grounds), `mark-mono-light` (`background` dark ink for light grounds), `mark-small` (single ink, no rings, no rim line, three field dots, strokes 3.2x: 24-48px).
- The mark's anatomy, kept in every variant: a dome with a raised ding and five tone fields; the rim, whose front edge and the bottom shell beneath it meet the dome at two points; three slightly flattened rings behind the pan, bright teal at the upper left and teal-to-gold below the pan, fading out elsewhere; one closed orbit tilted 16.4 degrees, teal dot at its lower-left tip, gold dot at its upper right, passing in front of the bottom shell and behind the dome.
- Lockups: `horizontal-*` (mark left; the app header and wide placements) and `stacked-*` (mark over wordmark over tagline; landing, OG image), each in full, single, mono-dark and mono-light. Wordmark and tagline are outlined paths, so they render without fonts.
- App icons, 1024px: `app-icon-full` and `app-icon-single` on `surface`, `app-icon-mono` on the light `background`.
- The app header uses the horizontal lockup between the link button (left) and the menu button (right), as in the phone mock.
- Minimum sizes: 64px tall for the web header lockup, 24px for the app icon. Below 48px use `mark-small` (it reads from 24px; a 16px favicon is still an open decision); never use the glow below 32px.
- Keep strokes thin and balanced; never recolour the orbit, never add colours, never place the full-colour glow on a light ground. Dot cores use tokens only: `accent-teal` for the teal dot, `warm-white` with an `accent-gold` bloom for the gold dot.

## Iconography

Line icons, 1.5–2px strokes in `text-primary`, inside `radius-md` buttons: link, menu (three bars), arrows, refresh. Match the mock; no filled icon sets, no emoji.
